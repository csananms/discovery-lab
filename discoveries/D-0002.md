# D-0002

**Date:** 2026-08-05

**Confidence:** Initial

**Evidence:**

- Multiple architecture sessions
- Consistent conceptual convergence

**Not yet demonstrated:**

- Real-world validation
- Multiple research programs
- External collaborators

## Observations

- Discovery Lab and AI-OS were initially discussed as separate projects.
- Through discussion, the two were described as forming an ecosystem.
- Discovery Lab was described as creating trustworthy knowledge.
- AI-OS was described as executing trustworthy operations.
- A Knowledge Domain and a continuous feedback loop were discussed as the connection between them.

## Interpretations

- The projects may be complementary rather than independent.
- The combined system may function as a scientist--AI ecosystem.
- The Knowledge Domain may be the shared medium through which knowledge and operations remain connected.

## Supported by Evidence

- The separation and later connection emerged during discussion.
- The roles of knowledge creation and operation execution were stated explicitly.

## Hypotheses

- A shared Knowledge Domain could improve continuity between discovery and execution.
- A feedback loop could help each system refine the other over time.
- The combined ecosystem could produce more trustworthy outcomes than either project alone.

## Architectural Consequences

- The repository should preserve discoveries about the system boundary, not just implementation details.
- Decisions may need to reference both knowledge generation and operational execution.
- Future work may need a clear shared vocabulary for the Knowledge Domain.

## Open Questions

- What exactly belongs in the Knowledge Domain?
- How are trustworthy knowledge and trustworthy operations validated?
- Where does the feedback loop begin and end?
- What evidence would show that the systems are truly coupled rather than merely adjacent?

## Possible Experiments

- Define a small Knowledge Domain artifact and trace one discovery through AI-OS execution.
- Compare a workflow with and without the shared domain.
- Test whether a discovery record changes the behavior of a later AI-OS operation.
- Look for failure modes where the feedback loop introduces confusion instead of trust.
