# Laboratory Charter

## Mission
- Explore and prototype small-scale research and engineering ideas.
- Validate novel concepts rapidly with lightweight experiments.
- Share learnings and reproducible artifacts openly.
- Lower the cost of exploration for project teams.
- Foster curiosity, rigor, and pragmatic iteration.
- Prioritize experiments that inform product or research decisions.
- Ensure ethical and inclusive practices in all work.

## Goals
- Produce at least one validated prototype per quarter.
- Document experiments with reproducible steps and results.
- Surface actionable insights to inform roadmap decisions.
- Maintain a portfolio of small demos and analysis notebooks.
- Encourage cross-functional collaboration and mentorship.
- Track outcomes and measure experiment impact.
- Continuously improve methods and tooling.

## Scope
- Short-term experiments (days–weeks) and feasibility studies.
- Prototypes, demos, evaluations, and technical write-ups.
- Public, open-source-friendly artifacts when possible.
- Not intended for full product feature development or long-term maintenance.
- Accepts proposals that align with mission and resource limits.
- Small teams (1–4 contributors) or individual projects encouraged.
- Each experiment must have clear success criteria and end date.

## Governance & Roles
- Lab Lead: steward of vision, resourcing, and prioritization.
- Project Owner: proposes and owns an experiment’s outcomes.
- Contributors: implement, test, and document experiments.
- Reviewers: provide critique, accept deliverables, and ensure quality.
- Decisions by lightweight consensus; Lab Lead breaks ties when needed.
- Regular retrospectives to refine process and criteria.
- Maintain a short approval checklist for new experiments.

## Initial Deliverables
- This Laboratory Charter (README).
- Experiment proposal and result templates.
- Starter repository of example prototypes and notebooks.
- One canonical experiment completed within the first month.
- Communication channel and meeting cadence established.
- Minimal CI for reproducible demo builds where helpful.
- Public summary of initial learnings after first milestone.

## Communication & Meetings
- Weekly 30-minute sync for status, blockers, and quick demos.
- Monthly show-and-tell for broader stakeholder updates.
- Use issues for proposals and PRs for deliverables.
- Maintain a dedicated chat channel (e.g., #discovery-lab).
- Keep meeting notes, decisions, and actions in this repo.
- Prefer short async updates over long meetings.
- Respect contributor time; meet only as necessary.

## Contribution Guidelines (short)
- File an issue with a concise experiment proposal.
- Complete the experiment template and assign an owner.
- Keep experiments small, time-boxed, and documented.
- Open PRs for deliverables with reproducible steps and artifacts.
- Credit collaborators and disclose dataset/IP constraints.
- Follow repo code of conduct and licensing guidance.
- Request Lab Lead support for resources beyond scope.
