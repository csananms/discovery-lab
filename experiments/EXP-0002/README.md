# EXP-0002: Trust Kernel Generalization

**Status:** Proposed

**Date:** 2026-08-06

## Research Question

Can one minimal Trust Kernel support multiple domains while preserving domain-specific meaning?

## Candidate Invariants

- Identity
- Lifecycle
- Provenance
- Evidence
- Transition Rules
- Preservation

## Current Evidence

- The candidate invariants emerged during a Mission 2 architecture session.
- No cross-domain implementation or validation has been completed.

## Hypothesis

A minimal Trust Kernel may support multiple domains while preserving domain-specific meaning.

## Potential Falsifiers

- A domain requires a foundational trust concept not represented by the candidate invariants.
- Applying the kernel removes or distorts meaning needed by a domain.
- The shared model adds more complexity than separate domain-specific models.

## Next Experiment

Model two bounded domains against the candidate invariants and record which concepts are shared, missing, or domain-specific.
