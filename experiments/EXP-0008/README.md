# EXP-0008: Tennis10 Child Development Research Intake

**Date:** 2026-08-09

**Status:** Examined

## Research Question

Can Discovery Lab preserve a Scientist-AI proposal for Tennis10 child-development research without treating the proposal as evidence of effectiveness, a validated measurement system, or an approved human-participant study?

## Input

- [REF-0006: Tennis10 Child Development Research Exploration](../../references/REF-0006.md)

## Evidence Boundary

The incoming source is a conceptual exploration. Its research questions, proposed tracks, and methodological cautions are source content. It contains no empirical results, validated instruments, or participant data. Laboratory reasoning below therefore concerns the proposal's evidential status, not Tennis10 outcomes.

## Classified Outputs

### Two Research Tracks Were Proposed

- **Classification:** Observation
- **Supporting evidence:** The source distinguishes an exploratory longitudinal single-learner track with an adapted program from a group study using a standardized program for junior players.
- **Confidence:** High for the source's stated proposal; no confidence is assigned to either track's feasibility or outcomes.
- **Lab reasoning:** The source does not assume that the two tracks use identical designs or analyses.

### Tennis10 May Function as an Adjustable Developmental Environment

- **Classification:** Hypothesis
- **Supporting evidence:** The source identifies adaptable equipment, court dimensions, task difficulty, rules, and social structure, and proposes repeated physical and social observations.
- **Confidence:** Initial. No intervention results, comparison condition, or validated measurement system was supplied.
- **Lab reasoning:** This is a testable research direction, not an established account of child development or disability support.

### Sport as a Measurable Development Environment

- **Classification:** Discovery Candidate
- **Supporting evidence:** The source proposes a structured sport setting in which task constraints and repeated observations could be documented together.
- **Confidence:** Initial. The proposal has not demonstrated measurement reliability, safety, feasibility, or developmental effect.
- **Potential architectural consequence:** None established. The existing reference and experiment records represent the proposal without a sports-, education-, or health-specific foundation.

### Measurement and Ethics Preconditions

- **Classification:** Open Question
- **Supporting evidence:** The source leaves test validity, age and disability appropriateness, operational definitions, observer reliability, consent, assent, privacy, safety, authorization, and ethics review unresolved.
- **Question:** Can a small, repeatable, ethically appropriate measurement approach be independently justified and piloted before any effectiveness claim is investigated?

## Protocol Assessment

| Concern | Result | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | Passed | The supplied exploration has a title, source type, and intake date. |
| Provenance | Passed with limitation | The source is identified as a Scientist-AI exploration; its original session is intentionally not retained. |
| Evidence | Passed | Proposal content, laboratory reasoning, hypotheses, and unanswered questions are separated. |
| Privacy and ethics | Workflow and research-governance constraint | No participant information was transferred; any future study must independently establish appropriate conditions. |
| Historical integrity | Passed | The proposal is preserved as an initial concept rather than retrospectively represented as completed research. |

## Items Not Transferred

- Any child, parent, coach, player, school, club, or site information.
- Any intervention, assessment, session, health, educational, or behavioral record.
- Any claim that a candidate test, observation scale, or Tennis10 Development Battery is validated.
- Any claim of effectiveness, developmental benefit, or suitability for disability support.

## Result

The existing Evidence Transfer Protocol can preserve this research concept without a domain-specific foundation or new architecture. The appropriate next action, if pursued, is an independently justified and ethically authorized research-design investigation; no study, measurement system, or effectiveness evaluation begins through this intake.
