# EXP-0021: Meet #3 Follow-on Observation — Participation to Professional Value

**Date:** 2026-09-01

**Status:** Proposed

## Research Question

Does repeated real-world evidence support a durable mechanism connecting
professional-development experiences to later professional outputs and
outcomes?

This is an intake question, not an established mechanism or validated model.

## Evidence Boundary

This record contains retrospective participant-reported context and a new
participant-reported observation. It is not independent verification of event
records, platform behavior, attendance, submission, learning, professional
capability, or professional growth.

## Retrospective Meet #3 Context

The following context is supplied retrospectively by the participant from the
earlier discussion. It was **not contemporaneously preserved in the Discovery
Lab repository**. It is included only because the new observation depends on
understanding what earlier experience it refers to.

- The participant took part in a constrained 45-minute Flood Rescue Hack.
- The resulting creative prototype was described as a “10-Minute Living Ark.”
- Codex was used across questioning/grilling, divergence, convergence, and
  execution.
- Guided-problem-solving was retained only as a candidate skill or hypothesis.
- The prototype was explicitly not treated as a validated flood-resilience
  solution because stakeholder and local-problem evidence was insufficient.
- The weekend trial was conversationally closed, but the experience and
  supporting evidence were not repository-preserved at that time.

This section does not recreate the prior conversation or promote its account
to contemporaneously preserved event evidence.

## New Participant-Reported Observation

After event check-in and one-shot challenge submission, there was a period in
which the participant had acted but did not yet possess a durable, personally
accessible record of:

- what had occurred;
- what had been submitted; or
- what the experience might later contribute to professional development.

This observation describes a reported experience of post-submission
durability and accessibility. It does not establish that event or platform
records were absent, lost, inaccessible to others, or unavailable through
external systems.

## Discussion Shift

The inquiry shifts from “How can we prove that a person really attended an
event?” toward “How can participation in events, training, and reskilling
become durable value for both an institutional member and the institution?”

The shift is a framing change for investigation, not evidence that such value
has been created or measured.

## Candidate Developmental Model

The following is a candidate model for investigation:

`Event / training → bounded participation evidence → learning / exposure → later application in real responsibility → output → outcome → possible evidence of professional growth`

The model is **unvalidated**. The intake supplies no longitudinal observations,
professional outputs, outcomes, comparison, or trusted organizational
assessment connecting the stages.
