## Evidence-Class Boundary

The transfer warrants retaining the following epistemic distinction at
Initial confidence:

`SYNTHETIC ≠ EXTERNAL_REPORTED ≠ AI/MODEL_OUTPUT ≠ LOCAL_PHYSICAL_OBSERVATION`

These are candidate evidence classes that may participate in related
scientific work while remaining epistemically distinct:

- **SYNTHETIC:** deliberately constructed test or fixture evidence;
- **EXTERNAL_REPORTED:** evidence or findings reported by an external source;
- **AI/MODEL_OUTPUT:** generated or inferred output attributable to an AI or
  model; and
- **LOCAL_PHYSICAL_OBSERVATION:** observation produced through local contact
  with a physical scientific process or specimen, as reported by the source.

The classes do not have automatic equivalence, corroboration, authenticity,
validity, or common evidentiary weight. Classification alone does not validate
an item, and this intake does not independently verify any source-project item
within these classes.
