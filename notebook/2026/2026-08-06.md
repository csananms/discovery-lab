# 2026-08-06

## Mission 2: Mid-Day Checkpoint

### Observations

- The Discovery Engine was reduced to six proposed capabilities: Capture, Register, Link, State, Review, and Preserve.
- Item, Investigation, and Knowledge were proposed as its three first-class entities.
- A single Discovery Inbox was proposed for all new ideas, observations, questions, external sources, and experiments before classification.
- Later discoveries may revisit earlier understanding; earlier artifacts are preserved rather than rewritten.

### Supported Architectural Conclusions

- The Discovery Engine now has a minimum conceptual boundary suitable for testing.
- Preservation of earlier artifacts is required for traceable revision of understanding.
- Challenge 2 is a proposed research experiment, not an accepted principle.

### Research Hypotheses

- The minimum kernel may be sufficient for a Discovery Engine.
- A minimal Trust Kernel may generalize across domains while preserving domain-specific meaning.
- The architecture may enable trustworthy, self-improving scientist-AI collaboration.

### Open Questions

- What are the minimum investigation states and evidence required for transition?
- How should the Discovery Inbox be classified without losing context?
- How should Item, Investigation, and Knowledge relate over time?
- What would validate or refute Trust Kernel generalization?
