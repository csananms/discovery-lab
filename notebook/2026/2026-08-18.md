## Bounded Evidence Reference Resolution Trial

The open question from [EXP-0014](../../experiments/EXP-0014/README.md) was tested with a small, explicitly laboratory-created fixture bundle for the synthetic temperature-check operation.

### Result

The input, result, operation record, and decision rule were internally consistent and reproduced the producer-reported `review_required` status. This demonstrates bounded reproduction when referenced contents are available.

The fixture is not independent producer evidence. Because Discovery Lab created it, the trial does not establish that the original application executed the operation, that the records are authentic, or that they are tamper-resistant.

### Classification

- **Observation:** resolved supporting records can reproduce one synthetic reported outcome.
- **Discovery Candidate:** evidence handoff may need a separate distinction between reproducibility and authenticity; this remains initial and unvalidated beyond one fixture.
- **Open Question:** what producer-generated provenance and authorization are sufficient for independent authenticity review?

### Boundary

No envelope field, ETP capability, schema, Trust subsystem, architecture, or accepted principle changed.
