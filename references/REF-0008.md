## Scope Boundary

The source describes exploratory development of a graduate-level Food Processing textbook. It reports an initial seven-chapter structure, a continuous orange-juice case, and iterative generation of draft educational material. It is historical process evidence, not a textbook, source literature, or verification of academic content.

## Human and AI Contribution Boundary

The source attributes educational purpose, structure, sequencing, assessment need, case selection, and subsequent direction to the human participant. It attributes draft generation and iterative expansion to AI. These source-stated roles are preserved without claiming a complete provenance record for every output.

## Limits

- No generated chapter, question, rubric, figure, reference, calculation, or technical claim is transferred as teaching knowledge.
- The source's account of usefulness and limitations is historical self-report, not independently evaluated evidence.
- The source does not establish graduate-level adequacy, scientific accuracy, or publication readiness.
